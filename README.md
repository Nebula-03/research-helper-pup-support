# Privacy Policy — Research Helper Pup

**Last updated: October 15, 2026**

Research Helper Pup is a Chrome extension that checks search-result and hovered-link URLs for safety warning signs and saves selected passages as research notes. It does not assess whether a search result matches your query. This policy explains what the extension accesses, why it accesses it, and where it is stored.

## Information the extension processes

- **Search-result links:** After you enable the research features, on Google, Bing, and DuckDuckGo results pages the extension checks up to 12 result destination URLs for safety. It does not read the submitted search query, result titles, or snippets, and it does not scroll to or highlight results.
- **Hovered links:** After you enable the research features, when you hover over a link on a non-search-results page, the extension checks that link’s destination URL using rules and a blocklist bundled with the extension. The URL is processed locally for that check.
- **Research notes:** Only when you use the Notes shortcut after enabling the research features, the extension captures the selected passage and saves it with the page title, page URL, and save time.

## Your consent

On first use, the extension shows a disclosure and asks you to choose **Enable these features** or **Not now** before it checks search-result or hovered-link URLs, or captures selected text. Choosing **Not now** leaves those features off. You can reopen the choice from the pup’s options menu by selecting **Enable research features**. Your choice is stored locally in your Chrome profile.

## Storage and sharing

Search-result destination URLs and hovered link URLs are processed locally for the features described above. The extension does not send them to a server. Link-check results may be kept temporarily in memory while the extension is running; they are not saved as browsing history.

Saved notes, your consent choice, and the pup sound mute setting are stored in Chrome’s local extension storage on your device. If you choose **Connect a local .txt file**, the extension can also write your notes to that file. The file is one you select, and its access handle is kept locally so the extension can write to it again. You can remove notes in the extension and disconnect the file from Notes.

The extension does not use an account, analytics, advertising, or a remote data service. It does not sell or share your information with third parties.

## Chrome Web Store Limited Use statement

Research Helper Pup uses information accessed through its permissions only to provide its disclosed features: checking search-result and hovered-link URLs, and saving passages that you choose to keep as notes. The extension does not use or transfer this information for advertising or unrelated purposes, and the developer does not allow people to read it, except where required by law or for security purposes.

## Your choices

- Enable or decline the research features at the first-use prompt. If you decline, choose **Enable research features** from the pup’s options menu to review the choice again.
- Mute or unmute the pup’s sounds from the pup’s options menu. This setting is saved in the current Chrome profile; text messages and animations continue while sounds are muted.
- Save a passage only when you use the Notes shortcut after enabling the research features.
- Delete a saved note using its delete control.
- Choose whether to connect a local `.txt` file, and disconnect it in Notes.
- Remove the extension to remove its Chrome-local notes. A `.txt` file you selected is a separate file on your device and is not deleted when you uninstall the extension.

## Permissions

- **Storage:** Used to keep your saved notes, consent choice, and sound mute setting in Chrome’s local extension storage.
- **Access to web pages:** Used, after consent, to check supported search-result destination URLs and hovered link destinations; display the pup; and save the current page details with a note. The extension does not read search queries, result titles, or snippets, and does not send page content or URLs to a remote service.
- **Access to a selected text file:** Granted only after you choose to connect a local `.txt` file, so the extension can write your notes there.

## Contact and support

For questions or support, open an issue at [Research Helper Pup Support](https://github.com/Nebula-03/research-helper-pup-support/issues). Issues are public. Please do not include passwords, private notes, personal information, or sensitive search terms or URLs.
